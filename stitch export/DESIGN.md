## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from high-caliber institutional European real estate, capturing the refined urban heritage of Barcelona combined with contemporary architectural discipline. It balances corporate stability, civic permanence, and Mediterranean warmth.

### Emotional Response & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:** High-net-worth real estate investors, corporate tenants, commercial leasing partners, residential buyers, and premium hotel patrons seeking permanence and aesthetic curation.
- **Tone & Mood:** Authoritative yet hospitable, architectural, grounded, and discerning. It eschews ephemeral startup trends in favor of timeless editorial gravity, structural order, and tactile luxury.

### Visual Style Movement
The design language adopts an **Architectural Editorial Modernism** framework:
- Generous structural whitespace echoing expansive gallery interiors and Mediterranean courtyard courtyards.
- Restrained, deliberate taxonomy with leading underscore signifiers (`_living`, `_working`, `_retail`, `_parkings`, `_hotels`).
- Subtle architectural line-work and warm stone-like surfaces that evoke limestone, travertine, and polished plaster rather than cold synthetic digital screens.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate institutional presence grounded in deep racing forest green and Mediterranean earthen pigments.

### Role Architecture
- **Primary (`#02362f`):** Dense British racing / forest green. Serves as the primary brand anchor, dominant headlines, high-priority buttons, key navigation bars, and structural markers.
- **Secondary (`#b45309` / `#d97706`):** Warm architectural ochre and terracotta. Used for precision accents, active tabs, subtle taxonomy indicator pips, and focused calls to action.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#f9f8f5` & `#f5f4f0`):** Limestone-inspired warm white and sandstone beige. Completely replaces harsh digital blue-gray slate backgrounds with warm natural mineral tones.
- **Surface Elevation (`#ffffff` & `#eae7e1`):** Pure alabaster cards layered against the sandstone canvas, defined with whisper-thin `#dedad2` structural borders.
- **Text & Hierarchy:** `#1a211f` provides high legibility with an organic charcoal cast that harmonizes with green hues, avoiding synthetic jet black.

## Typography

Typography fuses architectural precision with high-end European clarity:

- **Headlines & Taxonomy (`Outfit`):** Balanced, clean geometric grotesk that captures the clean proportions of European architectural signage and mid-century modern facades. Headings use controlled letter-spacing and lighter weights (`300` and `400` in oversized display sizes, scaling to `500`/`600` for sectional clarity).
- **Taxonomy Signifiers:** Specific business lines and functional pillars are set in lowercase with a prominent leading underscore (`_living`, `_working`, `_retail`, `_parkings`, `_hotels`) in `Outfit SemiBold`.
- **Body & Functional Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its open apertures, tall x-height, and neutral editorial readability, providing effortless scanning across real estate asset descriptions, specifications, and financial disclosures.
- **Metric Displays:** High-scale quantitative real estate stats (square meters, sustainability percentages, years of history) utilize `Outfit Light` at oversized scale (`48px+`) with razor-thin line weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model replicates architectural plans: structural discipline, generous negative space, and clear rhythmic division.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout with fixed max-width container of `1360px`, `gutter-lg` (`2.5rem` / `40px`), and outer canvas margin of `margin-lg` (`5rem` / `80px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) and margins of `margin-md` (`3rem` / `48px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `margin` (`1.5rem` / `24px`).

### Vertical Rhythm & Ratios
- Section transitions must use generous whitespace (`space-2xl` / `5rem` to `7rem`) to frame real estate imagery with gallery-like breathing room.
- Content groupings inside modules conform to an 8px modular baseline, utilizing `space-xs` (8px) for badge-to-title locks, `space-md` (20px) for paragraph separation, and `space-xl` (56px) between thematic property blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes surface material authenticity over heavy drop shadows. Depth is communicated through structural layering, ambient light tinting, and architectural borders.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Canvas (Base Ground):** Warm limestone `#f9f8f5`. Flat, unadorned foundation.
2. **Structural Inset / Secondary Panels:** Tinted warm sand `#f5f4f0` or rich brand forest container `#02362f` for high-impact brand statements.
3. **Elevated Cards (Level 1):** Solid `#ffffff` pristine alabaster cards with a delicate `1px solid #dedad2` perimeter outline and an ultra-diffused, ambient warm shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(2, 54, 47, 0.04)`.
4. **Hover & Interactive State (Level 2):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) with expanded ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(2, 54, 47, 0.08)`.
5. **Overlays & Modals (Level 3):** Frosted ambient backdrop (`rgba(2, 54, 47, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), supporting crisp pure white dialogue containers rimmed with warm hairline edges (`#dedad2`).

## Shapes

The geometric form language is tailored to high-end architectural discipline: clean, straight-edged structural containers mixed with soft perimeter rounding.

- **Primary Geometry:** `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius). Applied to image containers, editorial cards, property listings, and form inputs. This avoids the toy-like quality of heavy rounded corners while preventing the brutal harshness of raw 0px squares.
- **Pill Exception for Badges & Minor Controls:** Status badges, taxonomy pills, and contextual category markers strictly use full pill shapes (`9999px` radius) to juxtapose organic tactile stones against crisp architectural card rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid forest green background (`#02362f`), crisp white text, `4px` corner radius, `12px 28px` padding. On hover: shifts to rich terracotta ochre (`#b45309`) with a smooth `200ms ease-out` transition.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#02362f`, text in `#02362f`. Hover: fills with subtle tinted green wash (`rgba(2, 54, 47, 0.05)`).
- **Text / Editorial Link:** Styled with an architectural arrow (`Más información →`), set in `Outfit SemiBold`, featuring a terracotta hover color shift (`#d97706`) and an animated expanding bottom underline.

### Status Badges & Taxonomy Pills
- **Geometry:** Compact pill shape (`9999px` border radius) with `4px 12px` padding, set in uppercase `label-caps` (`11px`, `weight: 700`).
- **Domain Colors:**
  - `_living`: Light celadon background (`#e6efe9`), deep forest text (`#02362f`).
  - `_working`: Warm peach sand background (`#faebd7`), deep amber text (`#8c4408`).
  - `_retail`: Muted terracotta tint background (`#f9e8e5`), brick text (`#993826`).
  - `_parkings`: Slate mineral blue background (`#e6edf5`), dark nautical text (`#1b4468`).
  - `_hotels`: Vintage lavender stone background (`#ece8f1`), deep violet text (`#473264`).

### Real Estate Property Cards
- Pure white background (`#ffffff`), `1px` border in `#dedad2`, `4px` radius.
- Imagery is framed with zero border radius at top or an internal padded bezel, with an aspect ratio of `16:10` or `4:3`.
- Lower content module houses the taxonomy tag (e.g., `_living`), bold property title in `#02362f`, location subtitle in `#1a211f` (muted to 70%), and key quantitative specs (sqm, bedrooms, terrace) separated by hairline sandstone vertical dividers.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #dedad2`, `4px` radius, padding `12px 16px`. Text in `#1a211f`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to deep forest green (`#02362f`) with a subtle `2px` focus halo in `rgba(2, 54, 47, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp `2px` corners for checkboxes; circular for radios. Inactive border `#dedad2`; checked state fills solid `#02362f` with a pure white checkmark.

### Numerical Data Callouts
- Highlighted key company metrics (e.g., "75+ años", "100%", "27%") feature lightweight `Outfit` figures (`fontSize: 48px`, `fontWeight: 300`) in primary `#02362f`, flanked by brief explanatory paragraphs in `#1a211f` opacity 80%.
